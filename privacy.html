ChannelNest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

ChannelNest ("the app") is a media player. It does not provide, host, sell or recommend any TV channels, films, series or other content. You add your own playlist or service details, and the app plays what that source provides.

This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

Who we are

Gidiware (Gideon Paitoo), Accra, Ghana. Email: fpaitoo@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below stays on your device. We do not run servers that receive it, and we cannot see it.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of it. The app does not back this data up to the cloud (Android backup is disabled).

What the app sends over the network

  • To the source you added. The app connects directly to the server, playlist or EPG address you entered, sending the details needed to sign in and fetch channel lists, guides, artwork and streams. That provider's own privacy policy governs what they do with it. Many IPTV sources use unencrypted HTTP; when they do, this traffic is not encrypted in transit.
  • Chromecast (phone/tablet only). If you cast, the stream address is sent to your Cast device on your local network. Casting uses Google's Cast SDK (part of Google Play services). The Cast SDK sends Google anonymous, encrypted diagnostics about Cast device discovery and casting sessions, which Google uses in aggregate to improve casting. It contains no account, playlist or stream details, and it can't be turned off by the app. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No advertising or tracking SDKs, and no analytics of our own. The only diagnostics sent anywhere are the anonymous Cast SDK data described above.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Parental controls let you restrict what a profile can see, but content comes from the source you add.
